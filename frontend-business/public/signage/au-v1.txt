PLACEHOLDER — AUSTRALIA (Privacy Act 1988 / OAIC)
NOT FOR ACTUAL STORE DISPLAY
=====================================================

This file is a placeholder for the print-ready privacy signage
that businesses operating in Australia must display where TapProve
sensors are in operation.

The final version will be drafted with solicitor review against:
- Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the 2024 amendments
- Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), particularly APP 1, 3, 5
- Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)
  guidance on biometric and surveillance technology in retail

It should cover at minimum:
- Identity and contact details of the APP entity (the venue)
- Kinds of personal information collected (or, in our case,
  explicit confirmation that aggregate counts and hashed
  Bluetooth identifiers are collected — but no personal
  information as defined by the Act)
- Purposes of collection
- Consequences of not collecting (footfall analytics only —
  no impact on the customer's ability to enter the venue)
- Whether the information will be disclosed overseas (the
  TapProve backend is EU-hosted; this is relevant for AU disclosure)
- How to access, correct, or complain
- Reference to OAIC (oaic.gov.au) for unresolved complaints

Replace this file with the final signage PDF and bump the
SIGNAGE.AU.version entry in backend/services/signageVersions.js.
