This file is a placeholder for the print-ready privacy signage
that businesses operating in the UK must display where TapProve
sensors are in operation.

The final version will be drafted with solicitor review against:
- UK GDPR (Articles 13 & 14)
- Data Protection Act 2018 (UK)
- ICO guidance on CCTV, location tracking, and IoT in retail

It should cover at minimum:
- Who is the data controller (the venue) and who is the processor (TapProve)
- What is being measured (camera-based people counting, BLE / Wi-Fi
  signal counting, ambient noise level, ambient light)
- Confirmation that no video, photos, audio, or raw identifiers
  are recorded or transmitted
- Legal basis (legitimate interest)
- Retention periods
- How to exercise data subject rights
- Reference to ICO at ico.org.uk for complaints
- Contact details and link to tapprove.io/privacy

Replace this file with the final A5 / A4 PDF and bump the
SIGNAGE.GB.version entry in backend/services/signageVersions.js.
