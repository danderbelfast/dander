NOT FOR ACTUAL STORE DISPLAY
=====================================================

This file is a placeholder for the print-ready privacy signage
that businesses operating in New Zealand must display where
TapProve sensors are in operation.

The final version will be drafted with solicitor review against:
- Privacy Act 2020 (NZ)
- Information Privacy Principles (IPP), particularly IPP 1, 3
- Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC) guidance on
  biometric and IoT surveillance in retail (the OPC's 2024
  biometric processing draft code is particularly relevant)

It should cover at minimum:
- Name and contact details of the collecting agency (venue)
- What is being collected and how (visitor counting, BLE / Wi-Fi
  counting, noise / light)
- Confirmation that no personal information as defined by IPP 1
  is being collected (per the data audit)
- Why it's being collected (footfall analytics)
- Consequences of the customer choosing not to provide it
  (none — they can use the venue normally)
- Recipients of the information (the venue; TapProve as processor;
  aggregate anonymised analytics may be shared with researchers /
  local government bodies in future)
- Right to access and correct
- Contact for complaints, and reference to OPC (privacy.org.nz)

Replace this file with the final signage PDF and bump the
SIGNAGE.NZ.version entry in backend/services/signageVersions.js.
