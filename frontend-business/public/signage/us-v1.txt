PLACEHOLDER — UNITED STATES (CCPA-aligned baseline)
NOT FOR ACTUAL STORE DISPLAY
=====================================================

This file is a placeholder for the print-ready privacy signage
that businesses operating in the United States must display where
TapProve sensors are in operation.

US privacy law is a patchwork of state-level statutes (no federal
equivalent of GDPR). The baseline placeholder is drafted to satisfy
the most prescriptive of the major regimes (California CCPA /
CPRA), on the principle that what satisfies California typically
satisfies VA / CO / CT / UT.

Final version will be drafted with US privacy counsel review
against at minimum:
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and CPRA amendments
- Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act
- Colorado Privacy Act
- Connecticut Data Privacy Act
- Utah Consumer Privacy Act
- Federal Trade Commission Act §5 (deceptive practices)

It should cover at minimum:
- Notice at collection ("This venue uses sensors...")
- Categories of data being collected
- Purposes of collection
- Right to know, delete, correct, opt-out (CCPA)
- Right to limit use of sensitive personal information
- No sale of personal information (the baseline position; if you
  ever commercialise aggregate data, the wording changes
  significantly)
- Contact details for exercising rights
- Reference to tapprove.io/privacy

NOTE: California / Illinois may require additional disclosures
specific to facial recognition (BIPA in IL). TapProve doesn't run
facial recognition, but the placeholder includes a sentence
confirming this explicitly to head off ambiguity.

Replace this file with the final signage PDF and bump the
SIGNAGE.US.version entry in backend/services/signageVersions.js.
